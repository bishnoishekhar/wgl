Gas safety alert

If you smell gas, leave the area immediately. Do not try to find the source. From a safe location, call 911 and Washington Gas at 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427), option 1 (the Emergency Leak Line, available 24 hours a day).

Moving?

Tell us at least three business days ahead

Notify Washington Gas at least three business days in advance, not counting weekends and holidays, so your service is ready when you are.

Other pages on the Washington Gas website quote a shorter notice of at least 48 hours, so the notice period is not consistent. Three business days is the safer plan.

Start or stop service
