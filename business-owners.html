New service and equipment

Get natural gas for your business

Converting existing equipment to natural gas or adding gas equipment in a commercial space starts with working out how much gas you need.

  1. Determine your gas requirementVerify the MBTu per hour and the delivery pressure in psi of each gas appliance you will keep or add. You can do this yourself or ask your HVAC contractor, engineer or facilities manager.
  2. Complete the Service Information Request FormIt details your load, delivery pressure and general service requirements. For new construction, include a site plan, property plat and/or dimensional sketch showing the meter location, preferably in AutoCAD or PDF format.
  3. Submit the paperworkSend it to Washington Gas, Attention: Commercial Sales, 6801 Industrial Road, Springfield, Virginia 22151. List all anticipated gas equipment with its MBTu rating and required gas pressure.
  4. Find out the cost of the gas line and meterWashington Gas performs a cost-benefit analysis under the governing tariff in D.C., Maryland or Virginia.
  5. Sign and return the commitment letterReturn the customer commitment letter with any required contribution.
  6. Meter and installationWhen the underground service is complete, contact Washington Gas to request a meter. A Washington Gas underground contractor will contact you to schedule the gas line, and Washington Gas applies for the permits. The gas lines inside the property must be inspected by the local code authority, and your installer is responsible for the tie-in.

What does a gas line cost?

Costs vary. Washington Gas designs the job and gives a contribution quote, which takes about 14 to 21 days depending on site conditions. The Service Information Request Form is returned with a property plat or site plan showing the meter location.

Source: Washington Gas FAQs and the Add or Replace Equipment page.

Pages on the site differ on who to call first

The Add or Replace Equipment page says to contact an HVAC contractor of your choice if your facility needs 1.5 MBTu or less, and a Washington Gas Commercial Account Manager if it needs 1.5 MBTu or more. The Convert to Gas page lists the Telesales team (703-941-HEAT (4328)) for projects under 1.5 MBTU and 855-227-4173 for projects over 1.5 MBTU. If in doubt, use the numbers under Who to contact.

Added load or split meter

Tell Washington Gas if you add gas appliances so it can confirm your service and meter can handle the load. Complete the Added Load or Split Meter Request Form and send it by email or fax:

  • Email: 941sales@washgas.com
  • Fax: 703-750-7691

You will be notified if a service upgrade is needed.

What a split meter does

A split meter adds a new meter (or reinstalls the old one) with two regulators. New appliances get 2 PSIG and existing appliances get 7 inches water column, so each gets the right pressure.

The FAQ says contractors use one when it is cheaper or easier than tying into existing house piping, when the existing low-pressure piping is too small for the extra load, or when a new appliance needs higher pressure than the existing system can deliver.

Demolition, abandonment and temporary disconnection

Processing time
8 to 12 weeks, minimum

Requests are processed in the order received. Plan ahead.

Before you disconnect

  • For temporary disconnection during construction, call 844-WASHGAS to ask for the meter to be removed and the gas turned off at the meter.
  • Do not remove or discard the existing meter.
  • A temporary disconnect will most likely need a new underground gas service when you reconnect, because Washington Gas does not put previously existing gas piping back in service as a safety precaution.

Restarting service

Washington Gas will need your anticipated gas and pressure requirements, and a property plat or site plan with the new meter location marked, to get permits. The replacement service is subject to the governing tariff and a cost-benefit analysis, so a cost may apply.

To have Washington Gas confirm that a property has no gas service, complete the Request to Confirm No Gas Service Form and send it to the same email address or fax number. Questions about a pending new service connection: 941Sales@washgas.com.

Business energy solutions

Incentives for high-efficiency equipment

The Washington Gas Business Energy Solutions programmes offer rebates on high-efficiency natural gas equipment, custom building upgrades and free energy conservation kits for Maryland and Virginia commercial customers.

Check dates before you plan a Maryland project

The Maryland home rebate pages say the Washington Gas EmPOWER Maryland rebate programmes end on December 31, 2026 (see Rebates and Savings). The business pages read for this page give no end date of their own, so confirm timing at WGSmartSavings.com before you commit to a project. Specific amounts and requirements are on WGSmartSavings.com. Read on the Washington Gas website on October 17, 2026.

How to apply (Maryland and Virginia)

  1. Check eligibilityConfirm your project meets the requirements in the applicable Technical Sheet on the online application center.
  2. Apply for pre-approvalYou need the Washington Gas account number for the project site, the customer-signed Terms and Conditions form, manufacturer specification sheets for all proposed measures, and a detailed cost breakdown or itemized estimates for Custom projects.
  3. Wait for approvalA technical review team reviews the application, and a pre-inspection may be required. After pre-approval, existing equipment can be removed and new equipment bought and installed. Tell the programme right away about any change to scope or timeline.

Equipment must be installed by a licensed service provider registered with the Washington Gas Business Energy Solutions programme for the project location. Rebates are payable only to the name on the Washington Gas account and records.

Timing: rebates are processed within six to eight weeks of a complete application. A share of applications is audited, which can add three to six weeks. If 12 weeks have passed since you mailed your submission, call 1-833-286-0860.

D.C. businesses: the FAQ points to the D.C. Sustainable Energy Utility at dcseu.org for current rebates.

Free Commercial Energy Conservation Kit (Maryland)

Answer a few survey questions to see if you qualify for a complimentary kit, which arrives by mail in six to eight weeks. It includes pipe insulation, faucet aerators, switch and outlet sealing gaskets, spray foam sealant, squeeze tube caulk, adhesive weatherstrip and a door sweep, plus a spray valve for food service businesses only.

Paying a business bill by card

Washington Gas adds a convenience fee when you pay a bill by credit or debit card: $9.95 for commercial. You can charge up to $5,000 per transaction with the $9.95 fee. The fee is not charged on eCheck, personal check or cash, and there is no limit on the eCheck option in the My Washington Gas app.
