Home owners

Up to $800 with a participating contractor

The Washington Gas FAQ says Maryland and Virginia customers can save up to $800 with Home Energy Savings Program rebates. You need a participating contractor in the Home Energy Savings Contractor Network, and self-installed appliances do not qualify.

Maryland rebates end December 31, 2026. Submit by December 1.

The Washington Gas EmPOWER Maryland rebate programmes end on December 31, 2026, as required by Maryland House Bill 1532. The site advises submitting the application and all documents by December 1 to help make sure review and payment happen before the programmes end. Rebates are paid within six to eight weeks of a complete application. Virginia programmes are not affected. Read on October 17, 2026.

The amounts differ by page

The FAQ page says “up to $800”, while the programme pages below list larger amounts for some equipment (for example $900 for a Maryland combination heating unit and up to $2,000 on select Virginia boilers and furnaces). Another page lists a Virginia furnace rebate of $150 and a smart thermostat rebate of $50. Confirm amounts at WGSmartSavings.com before buying.

How a Maryland rebate works

  1. Choose a participating contractorMaryland customers must use a contractor in the contractor network.
  2. The contractor installs and appliesYour contractor installs the qualifying high-efficiency equipment and submits the rebate application for you.
  3. Receive the rebateOn the contractor invoice or by check, within 6 to 8 weeks of a completed application.

Washington, D.C.

The website gives no current Washington Gas rebate programme or dates for D.C. The FAQ page says Washington Gas programmes were pending approval for 2024, which is out of date. It says D.C. residents can get rebates from the District of Columbia Sustainable Energy Utility (DCSEU) at dcseu.com, and D.C. businesses are pointed to dcseu.org.

The WGSmartSavings D.C. page describes Washington Gas rebates on high-efficiency equipment in general terms, and says manufacturer rebates can be combined, but gives no amounts or dates. The home energy assessment is also marked pending approval for D.C. customers.

Limited-time offer

$100 off select Rinnai tankless water heaters

Available to customers in Maryland, D.C. and Virginia, with one claim per household.

  • Buy a qualifying product between September 1 and December 31, 2026.
  • Installation must be done by January 15, 2027.
  • The claim is due by February 15, 2027.

Dates as read on October 17, 2026. Visit WGSmartSavings.com for current offers.

Find a participating contractor

Rebates need installation by a participating contractor in the Home Energy Savings Contractor Network (the Maryland page calls it the EmPOWER Contractor Network). Washington Gas has verified participating contractors’ applicable trade licenses and insurance. It does not guarantee dealers’ or installers’ work and recommends that you check references.

If your contractor is not listed, ask them to join the network. To qualify for the high-efficiency rebates, the equipment must be installed by a participating contractor.

Business owners

Maryland and Virginia commercial customers can get rebates on high-efficiency equipment, custom building upgrades and free conservation kits. D.C. businesses are pointed to dcseu.org.

Builders and contractors

The Maryland and Virginia builder update page says builder incentives are currently approved through May 1, 2028. The Maryland New Home Construction Program offers rebates on high-efficiency equipment to Maryland and Virginia homebuilders, with technical training and sales and marketing support (details at WGSmartSavings.com/Builder).
