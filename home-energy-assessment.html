Home Energy Assessment

An online tool that gives you personalised tips and recommendations for saving energy in your home. It takes just a few minutes.

What it isHow to startRelated savings

What the assessment is

The Home Energy Assessment on the Washington Gas website is an online tool. You tell Washington Gas about your home, and it gives you personalised tips and recommendations for saving energy.

D.C. customers

The assessment page marks it as pending approval for D.C. customers. Its state selector offers Virginia and Maryland. The WGSmartSavings D.C. page mentions an Online Home Energy Profile with a possible free Energy Conservation Kit depending on survey responses, so the pages are not consistent for D.C.

Is there a reward?

The Virginia rebate page says completing a home energy profile earns an energy conservation kit.

How to start

  1. Open the assessmentIt opens on a separate Washington Gas page.
  2. Choose your stateSelect the state you live in.
  3. Tell us about your homeAnswer the questions about your home to get your tips and recommendations.
Start the assessment (opens in a new tab)

The assessment is run on the Washington Gas Opower page (wgl.opower.com), not on this demonstration site.
