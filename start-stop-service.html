Start or Stop Service

Moving, starting service or closing an account? Here is what to know before you request it.

How to requestNoticeFeesRequest form (demo)Customer ChoiceNew service

How to start or stop service

You can start or stop service by calling 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427) or by submitting the request online through the Washington Gas customer portal.

  • A fee applies to start service, and a security deposit may be required.
  • Electric service must be active before gas can be turned on for electronic ignition appliances.
  • If you do not request a stop, your account may stay open and you stay responsible for charges.
  • Someone must be able to give access to the home.

How much notice?

Notice periods are not consistent across the site

The moving FAQ says to notify Washington Gas at least three business days in advance, not counting weekends and holidays. The start and stop page says to give at least two business days or 48 hours notice, excluding weekends and holidays. Three business days is the safer plan.

If your landlord or property manager is enrolled in the Automatic Name Change Program (ANCP), the account can transfer to you without interruption. Submit a move-in order on the customer portal within 48 hours before move-in.

Customer Choice

All customers in Washington D.C., Maryland and Virginia can choose a licensed energy supplier, and enrollment is open every month. You do not have to buy gas from Washington Gas. Washington Gas still delivers the gas, reads meters, bills and collects, and responds to emergencies around the clock, whichever supplier you pick. Suppliers do not work for Washington Gas; they compete for your business.

Customer Choice details and questions to ask suppliers

Service in your name

Call 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427) to speak with a customer service representative about getting an account in your name. Account information is shared only with the named account holder, who alone can authorize adding another name.

Get natural gas: new service, meters and appliances

Cost of running a gas line

Costs vary. Complete the Service Information Request Form and return it with a property plat or site plan showing the meter location. Washington Gas designs the job and gives a contribution quote, which takes about 14 to 21 days depending on site conditions. For projects of one or two homes, call 703-941-HEAT (4328) or email 941sales@washgas.com.

Getting a meter installed or moved

To set up an account and schedule the meter set, call 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427). Washington Gas cannot set the meter until your local code authority approves the gas inspection.

To move an inside meter outside, call 844-WASHGAS (927-4427) and request an appointment for a relocation estimate.

Adding gas appliances or a split meter

Tell Washington Gas if you add gas appliances so it can confirm your service and meter can handle the load. Complete the Added Load or Split Meter Request Form and email it to 941sales@washgas.com or fax it to 703-750-7691. You will be notified if a service upgrade is needed. A split meter adds a new meter with two regulators so new and existing appliances get the right pressure.

Demolition or temporary disconnection

If you are demolishing a building or need gas temporarily disconnected for construction, complete the Gas Service Abandonment or Demolition Request Form. Email it to svcabandonments@washgas.com or fax it to 703-750-7570. A new service line will likely be needed when you restart service.

Damage claims

To report damage you believe Washington Gas caused, complete and submit the Damage Claim Form online with photos or invoices. You choose a claim type of property restoration, reimbursement or auto claim. The site says it will respond within 48 to 72 hours, and you may be asked for more information or an on-site inspection. There is no strict filing deadline, but file as soon as possible. Paving restoration typically takes 45 to 60 days after services are done. Contact: 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427).
