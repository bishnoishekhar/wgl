Gas emergency

If you smell gas, leave the area immediately. Do not try to find the source. From a safe location, call 911 and Washington Gas at 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427), option 1 (the Emergency Leak Line, available 24 hours a day).

Frequently asked questions

Who can see or change my account information?

Washington Gas shares account information only with the named account holder. Only the named account holder can authorize adding another name to the account. To revise account information or add an authorized name, call 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427).

What do the charges on my bill mean?

The customer charge is a fixed monthly amount that covers certain costs of providing service. The distribution charge is based on the amount of gas you use, shown in therms, and can include surcharges such as PROJECTpipes in D.C., STRIDE in Maryland and riders in Virginia. The gas charge appears as PGC on your bill, and taxes are collected for your local jurisdiction. The website lists no dollar amounts.

What is the purchased gas charge (PGC)?

If you buy gas from Washington Gas, you see PGC on your bill. It covers the gas supply and interstate pipeline transport, and Washington Gas does not profit from the gas sale. Washington Gas charges regulated customers the same price it pays for the gas, and its per-therm price changes monthly.

What is the Weather Normalization Adjustment (Virginia only)?

It adjusts for the weather of the previous winter heating season, November to May, and is not based on how much gas you used: colder than normal gives a credit and warmer than normal gives a surcharge. The adjustment may be split over up to three bills.

Can I choose my gas supplier?

Yes. See Customer Choice.

Is there a Spanish-language page?

Washington Gas has a Spanish-language information page at washingtongas.com/espanol. It lists the same customer service number, 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427), and the same gas emergency line, option 1.
