Washington Gas Cares

If you are facing financial hardships, we can help. Find assistance programmes, payment plans and protections.

Help is available

Washington Gas Cares points customers to state and local energy assistance programmes, the Washington Area Fuel Fund, and bill tools such as installment plans, the Budget Plan, eBill and AutoPay.

For energy assistance questions, email EAP@washgas.com or call 703-750-4406. Customer service is 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427).

Not sure where to start?

Our assistant can point you to the right programme.

Bill tools and protections

Budget Plan (level pay)

The Budget Plan spreads winter heating costs across the year so you pay about the same amount each month, based on past usage adjusted for normal weather and projected gas costs. The 12th bill includes a final adjustment, and any overpayment is credited to your account unless you ask for a refund. A late or missed payment can remove you from the plan, and then the full balance is due.

To enroll, go to washingtongas.com/budget-billing, log in to your online account, use the My Washington Gas app, or call 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427).

Payment plans

An installment plan lets you pay a past-due balance over up to 12 months, or up to 24 months if you receive energy assistance. See the payment plans details or call 844-WASHGAS and choose option 2.

Gift of Warmth

Gift of Warmth lets you help a friend, family member or neighbor pay a gas bill. You can also give to the Washington Area Fuel Fund. Details are at washingtongas.com/get-assistance/washington-gas-cares/give-assistance.

Medical Certification Program

A residential customer whose household member has a serious illness or relies on life-support equipment can ask for medical certification. Call 844-WASHGAS right away if you cannot pay. Then complete the Medical Certification form for your jurisdiction. A licensed physician, certified nurse practitioner or physician assistant must certify it.

Disconnection is postponed for 30 days after the form is received, and in that time you must enroll in an installment plan or pay the balance.

Termination notice

Washington Gas must send a written notice at least 14 days before a scheduled termination, except in emergencies or suspected meter tampering. If you have trouble paying, tell Washington Gas right away, because payment plans are available. Call 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427) to pay the minimum balance or arrange a payment plan.

The website gives no late fee or deposit amounts and does not describe a general winter shutoff rule.
