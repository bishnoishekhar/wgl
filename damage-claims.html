Damage Claims

If you believe Washington Gas work damaged your property, you can file a claim for restoration or reimbursement.

How to fileAfter you fileQuestions and answersWhat the site does not say

How to file a damage claim

Washington Gas says it is committed to addressing property damage that may occur as a result of its work. To start a claim, complete and submit the Damage Claim Form online.

  1. Complete the online formThe form asks for your contact details, service address, whether you are a Washington Gas customer, the date of damage, and a description of how the damage occurred.
  2. Choose a claim type and locationClaim types are Property Restoration, Reimbursement or Auto Claim. Location of damage is Lawn, Street/Paving or Other.
  3. Add photos or invoicesInclude photos of the damage and any invoices or receipts for repairs or replacements. If you already made repairs, you can still file: include photos of the original damage and copies of receipts.
  4. Watch for the confirmation emailYou receive a confirmation email once your form has been received.
Open the official Damage Claim Form (opens in a new tab)

This demonstration site has no claim form. The form is on the official Washington Gas page.

Smell gas? This is not the claims process.

Leave the area immediately, then call 911 and 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427), option 1. Do not try to find the source.

Quick facts

  • First response within 48 to 72 hours after your claim is received.
  • No strict filing deadline, but file as soon as possible after the incident.
  • Paving restoration typically takes 45 to 60 days after services are done.

After you file

48 to 72 hours

Initial response

The team reviews the details and responds within 48 to 72 hours. You may be asked for more information or to schedule an on-site inspection, depending on the nature and severity of the damage.

Resolution

Timeline varies

The timeline for final resolution or restoration depends on the scope and complexity of the damage. Paving restoration typically takes 45 to 60 days after services are done, because contractors must be scheduled and permits obtained, which can depend on weather and local regulations.

Reimbursement

Case by case

Reimbursements are evaluated case by case. Approved claims are reimbursed based on documentation, scope of responsibility and company policy, so you will not necessarily be reimbursed for all expenses.

Questions and answers

What qualifies as a valid damage claim?

Property restoration such as paving, landscaping or home repairs, and reimbursement requests for personal property damaged as a result of company-related activity.

What documentation do I need?

Photos of the damage, and invoices or receipts for related repairs or replacements.

Is there a deadline?

There is no strict deadline, but the site recommends submitting your claim as soon as possible after the incident to ensure timely review and resolution.

Will someone come to inspect the damage?

Depending on the nature and severity of the claim, a representative may schedule a site visit to inspect the damage before restoration or reimbursement.

Who do I contact with questions about my claim?

Once your claim is submitted, a representative from the appropriate department will reach out to you. For questions or updates, contact the same representative or department that first contacted you. The page lists 844-WASHGAS (844-927-4427) for customer service.

What the site does not say

The Damage Claims page offers the online form and the 844-WASHGAS customer service number only. It does not list a claims email address, a mailing address, a fax number or a claim status page, and it does not mention any fee. Official Damage Claims page (opens in a new tab).
